# Portfolio Evidence

Build professional claims from verifiable artifacts.

1. Establish repository ownership, visibility, upstream or fork status, license, contributor history, and the user's actual contribution.
2. Identify languages, frameworks, infrastructure, tests, CI, documentation, releases, and recent activity from files and history.
3. Separate authored work, adaptations, maintenance, collaboration, and third-party foundations.
4. Extract defensible evidence: commits, modules, issues, deployments, architecture decisions, operational outcomes, and documentation.
5. Flag secrets, proprietary material, missing attribution, stale dependencies, and claims that cannot be published safely.
6. Recommend `highlight`, `modernize`, `document`, `archive`, or `keep private` with reasons.
7. Generate bilingual descriptions appropriate to the evidence and target role.

Never invent metrics, users, production scale, ownership, or outcomes. Output an evidence table, risk notes, recommendation, and reusable English and Spanish project summary.
